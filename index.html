dryport — rail-yard simulation, audited and corrected in the open flagship

A discrete-event simulation (Python/SimPy) of a rail-served container yard, replayed live in 3D (Three.js). Partway through the build, I stopped and checked the simulation's own assumptions against outside data — everything below is what that check turned up.

  • Ran a systematic audit of all 57 constants in the simulation against industry data (BLS wage data, EPRI equipment studies, PierPASS terminal statistics, federal rail regulation). Found six calibration errors of 2×–99× in the core assumptions — a trolley speed sourced from the wrong crane type, a demand model built on marine free-time figures misapplied to a rail ramp.
  • Retested every published finding that depended on the corrected constants. Two changed. One algorithm's "harmful" verdict disappeared once the trains were sized correctly. A stacking strategy shown winning "at every noise level" turned out to lose at the noisiest end once retested.
  • The corrections sit next to the original numbers in the project's own changelog.

gladiator-arena — a live viewer that caught a bug in minutes public repo

An autonomous D&D-style combat simulator — line-of-sight, per-class movement AI, personality traits that drive behavior — paired with a live 3D viewer built on Three.js. Both were built in a single session, one immediately put to use watching the other.

  • The viewer shipped, and a match froze within minutes of watching it run. That observation led to a full audit, cross-checked against 5,246 archived matches from the project's own production history.
  • Found a class that could almost never land a hit: an off-by-one in its approach logic, live for months, averaging 0.05–0.17 kills/match against 0.40–0.85+ for every other class, never previously flagged. Fixed and re-verified at 0.28–0.55.
  • Found that every stalemate had been silently recorded as a decisive win, confirmed in 408 of 408 cases, quietly corrupting the leaderboard. Fixed in both engines.
  • Sourced CC0 3D character assets by driving itch.io's actual interactive download flow through headless Chrome, end to end, since there was no plain download URL to fetch.

Sports forecasting — no hindsight allowed public repo

An Elo-rating forecaster for MLB and NBA with one rule that never bends: every prediction in the accuracy ledger used only data available before that game — no adjusting after the fact.
